 Best Current Practices for Publication of DS Automation Events in RDAP

Abstract

   This document describes the best current practices for the
   publication of events regarding Delegation Signer (DS) records in
   RDAP domain registrations as described in RFC10026.

1.  Introduction

   [RFC10026] is a BCP regarding operational practices around the
   automation of maintenance of Delegation Signer (DS) DNSSEC resource
   records.  This document describes how that information should be
   published in RDAP.

2.  Conventions and Definitions

   The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT",
   "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and
   "OPTIONAL" in this document are to be interpreted as described in
   BCP 14 [RFC2119] [RFC8174] when, and only when, they appear in all
   capitals, as shown here.
